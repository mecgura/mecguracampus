How It Works — 3 Levels

A complete multi-tenant SaaS model

👑

Level 1 — You (Super Admin)

The owner of MecguraCampus. Add schools, assign subscription plans, track payments, and activate or suspend any tenant in one click.

🏫

Level 2 — School (Tenant)

Each school gets an isolated login and database. No school can ever see another school's data. One codebase, hundreds of schools.

📊

Level 3 — Users

Principal, teacher, accountant, parent, student and driver — role-based access, so everyone sees exactly what they need.
